## Eight core actions: actual artwork dependency

The agreed core actions are **Idle, Bounce, Jump, Celebrate, Greet, Sleepy, Surprised, Encourage**. Walking is an additional request, not a substitute for any of these eight. A full turn is optional in celebration and cannot be faked as a genuine 3D/rear view with frontal artwork.

| Core action | What existing kit artwork can support | Remaining proof / actual art gap |
|---|---|---|
| Idle | Body breathing; separate eye switching; headwear/sprout movement where separated | Full loops and readability need QA. Summer primary optics block blink; optional v2 lens requires approval. Hat+sprout groups cannot separately sway sprout. |
| Bounce | Small compression and two low assembled-body bounces | No mandatory new art for modest 2D motion. Ground contact and costume distortion need actual playback review. Not equivalent to independent limb motion. |
| Jump | Assembled anticipation/ascent/landing with local parts attached | No mandatory new art for bounded frontal jump. Every prop/shoulder must survive combined extremes; pillow/coat/helmet rigidity and foot visibility need QA. Deep crouch would need alternate anatomy. |
| Celebrate | Separate raised wings, low torso wiggle, closed-eye happy pose | Exact happy mouth absent as separate layer in selected manifests. Can keep approved smiling beak; new mouth required only for changing expression. Genuine turn requires new views. Pillow/grips restrict two-free-hand raises. |
| Greet | One free wing wave and slight assembled lean | Prop hand must retain its grip; Cafe/Summer/Halloween use free side, Fitness/Pajamas need constrained gesture or authored release pose. No silently detached hand/prop. |
| Sleepy | Closed/half eyes and slow assembled nod | Cafe/Student need matched half lids if gradual eye transition is required; Summer needs readable optics; new mouth needed for yawn. Lying asleep is a separate body pose, not obtained by rotating standing Pio. |
| Surprised | Quick recoil, slight expansion, brief hold with existing open eyes | Enlarged-eye or changed-mouth expression needs authored states. Current sources do not provide those independent expressive replacements. Silhouette/timing alone can support the specified core recoil. |
| Encourage | Gentle nod, modest wing opening, hold then recovery | Both hands cannot freely open while retaining a pillow/two weights. Use bounded grip-preserving gesture, or author released/supporting hand poses. No new torso needed for a small frontal lean. |
