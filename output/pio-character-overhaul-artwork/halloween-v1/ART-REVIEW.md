# Halloween layered candidate — artwork review

**PARTIAL — unapproved reconstruction, no app integration.** Five newly generated source roles (body, combined hat+sprout, left wing, right hand, basket) and eight registrations of genuine shared eye/eyelid/foot art make 13 full-canvas transparent derivative layers. Reused eye and foot registrations are not newly authored artwork. Original approved Halloween PNG is unchanged.

Native 1254×1254 top-left coordinates preserve the new body identity transform. Other whole sources are uniformly downscaled and rigidly translated/rotated; no crops, masks, alpha cleanup, retouching, geometric replacements or upscaling. Source SHA-256, pixel dimensions, transforms, z-order and pivots are recorded in registration/registration-candidate.json and registered-layers/manifest.json. Open/half/closed eye layers are mutually exclusive alternatives. The combined cap/sprout is a single part.

Actual full-size neutral light/dark, closed-eye dark, left-wing ±12° light/dark, raised-basket dark and 64/220pt3x original/candidate boards were inspected. The first hat fit covered the right eye; final registration shrinks the whole hat 5%, rotates it −6° and translates it upward 25px relative to the initial fit. Both eyes remain visible. The left wing root is hidden behind the torso and retains coverage at the inspected endpoints. Its shoulder pivot is (300,850). The right hand and basket share the exact shoulder pivot (970,840), with a common −8° endpoint; the basket renders below the hand, so the handle remains in its grasp. Body/headwear stay fixed while eyes switch, the left wing rotates and the hand/basket move in the 48-frame, 512-square offline GIF. This proves separated artwork motion in the compositor only, not native application animation.

There are 19 actual QA PNGs plus 13 registered transparent layer PNGs and one GIF. png-verification.json decodes and hashes every PNG; source-alpha-audit.json records genuine transparent/partial/opaque pixels. Expanded-canvas diagnostics find zero alpha≥16 pixels outside 1254² for all parts. The rotated headwear has 9 sub-alpha16 pixels outside the canvas; this negligible low-alpha footprint is explicitly recorded rather than silently reported as zero clipping.

Differences needing visual approval: the reconstructed hat rim and sprout silhouette, body costume folds, shared eye highlights and foot perspective differ from the approved flattened PNG. Left wing uses two visible rounded lobes and a rear shoulder position rather than the exact original foreground palm. The holding hand is a separate smooth palm resting over the handle, not a detailed grasp reconstruction. These are candidates, not pixel-identical source extractions. The intended color/material language is preserved, but original visual approval does not transfer automatically.

Half-eye artwork has been exported with provisional registration; only full open/closed states were visually inspected. Eye transitions, arbitrary combined joint angles, walking/jumping/landing, Reduce Motion, native Mac/iPhone, growth variants and performance remain NOT_TESTED.
