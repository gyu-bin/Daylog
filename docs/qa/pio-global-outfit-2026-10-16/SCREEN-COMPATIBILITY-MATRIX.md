## Artwork and motion limits

Five composed scenes lack matching art for every premium outfit: **13 × 5 = 65 missing premium scene variants**. Expected identifiers are the corresponding existing scene asset name suffixed with `_<outfitID>`. They are missing-art requirements, not files generated by this integration. No upright catalog image is overlaid onto a hammock, reclining body or tablet-holding scene.

All 14 current references and the four optional layered pilots represent one authored appearance each. The application retains levels and stage thresholds 1/10/25/50, but `growthCompatible` remains false. Four different stage numbers do not establish four distinct character artworks. Optional layered pilots are Basic, Christmas, Hanbok and Astronaut; their availability requires the separate DEBUG layered-rig opt-in. They do not make the other ten catalog entries independently rigged.

Reduce Motion, scene activity and view visibility remain the renderer's responsibility. The [native motion review](../routimori-storekit-2026-10-16/NATIVE-MOTION.md) verifies one Christmas 220 pt jump in a real simulator recording and its actual-system Reduce Motion gate: active native scene, zero active rigs/tracks when the setting is on. It does not prove every outfit/scene's motion or production lifecycle. Older OS versions without the scroll-visibility API retain appear/disappear and scene/window guards, so an all-version scroll-offscreen guarantee still requires separate evidence. The test host reads real Reduce Motion and bridges only its attached native scene's actual lifecycle; it does not force always-active rendering.

## Verified test evidence and remaining gates

Read [mac-global-unit-v2.log](../routimori-storekit-2026-10-16/mac-global-unit-v2.log): the final Swift Testing result is **43 tests in four suites passed**, followed by `TEST SUCCEEDED`, with result bundle `/tmp/Routimori-global-unit-v2.xcresult`. Suites are `PioAppearanceTests`, `PioPurchaseTests`, `PioWardrobeTests`, and `PioAppIconTests`. This includes live Observation invalidation, stage boundaries, all catalog mappings, preview isolation, ownership fallback, composed-scene limitations and existing purchase fixtures. It is a positive Mac focused unit result, not a native screenshot/motion pass and not real StoreKit transaction verification.

The iPhone Apple-sheet purchase, cancellation, explicit restore and process relaunch sequence is **PASS (native flow)** within Xcode's local StoreKit environment, as detailed in [NATIVE-REVIEW.md](../routimori-storekit-2026-10-16/NATIVE-REVIEW.md). This does not establish production Sandbox/App Store payment readiness. That record explicitly leaves Mac interactive purchase confirmation reverification pending.

The global screenshots establish **PASS (visual)** for the linked hosted Mac/iPhone growth, upright and splash views. Halloween remains selected across the iPhone splash, vacation day/night and all three cloud previews; the composed scenes honestly display Basic with a compatibility notice, so those costume applications remain **PARTIAL**. Screenshot selection and hosting do not establish an end-to-end route from purchase through every app screen or genuine cloud completion/error callbacks; those complete flows remain **NOT_TESTED**.

QA host preference safety was corrected: `PioGlobalReviewView` now creates `DevicePreferences` in a stable `@State`, backed by a unique `DaylogStoreKitTests.PioGlobalReview.<UUID>` defaults suite and removes only that suite on disappearance. Welcome-card dismissal therefore cannot write the application's shared preferences in the updated harness. The earlier screenshots are visual evidence, not proof that this later isolation correction ran; no Welcome dismissal was performed before the correction, per the coordinating review.

The native-host lifecycle diagnosis is confirmed in [ios-native-motion-review.log](../routimori-storekit-2026-10-16/ios-native-motion-review.log): the manually hosted view inherited `background` while its real native scene was `active`. The test-only bridge restored correct scene delivery. [The actual Christmas jump clip](../routimori-storekit-2026-10-16/ios-native-christmas-jump.mp4) and 758 character-region samples / 389 unique hashes provide motion evidence. Identical resting-phase screenshot pairs and the initial unbridged static video are not motion proof. The Reduce Motion screenshot taken after the ten-minute test timeout is invalid; only the valid on-state screenshot and matching zero-track diagnostics are cited. Full details, exclusions and the unbenchmarked CPU/RSS observation are in [NATIVE-MOTION.md](../routimori-storekit-2026-10-16/NATIVE-MOTION.md).

Global visual compatibility remains **PARTIAL**. iPad layouts, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, all-scene/all-outfit Reduce Motion, resume after switching it off, offscreen/background lifecycle, native stage transitions and all-outfit/revocation propagation still require evidence. Whole-application navigation and genuine cloud callbacks remain **NOT_TESTED**. No release-ready claim is made.
